Contact support

jswisher@mednet.ucla.edu

HERO is operated by Jeremy Swisher. Describe what happened, the screen you were using, your app version and your device and operating-system version. If an error appears, include its wording. Do not send your password, sign-in code, access token or medical records. Response timing is not guaranteed.

Plans and accounts

You can use the guest workspace without an account. Create a HERO account to use a separate saved account workspace. If you are waiting for a signup confirmation email, check your inbox and spam folder. Once your email is confirmed, return to HERO and sign in with your password.

Where offered, you can also choose Continue with Google or Continue with Apple, on the web and in the iOS app. Use the same choice each time. If a choice opens an empty workspace, sign out and sign in the way you did before. If you chose Hide My Email with Apple, Your account shows your private relay address; include that address when you contact support about your account.

In a browser tab, HERO stays signed in until you close the tab or sign out. If you add HERO to your home screen or install it as an app, it stays signed in on that device until you sign out. On a shared computer, use a browser tab, sign out when you finish and clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Review account and sync status in Your account. If sync reports a conflict, review the saved account version before replacing your current plan. Signing out can leave an account-specific offline workspace. Keep unsynced changes on your device until you no longer need them.

If you cannot sign in, contact support for help. Never send a password or sign-in code. For account deletion, use Your account > Delete my account, review the notice and confirm. If device cleanup needs another attempt, choose Retry device cleanup. Contact support if you cannot access the account. The privacy notice explains deletion and offline copies.

To remove HERO's Google sign-in connection, remove HERO from the third-party apps and services connected to your Google Account, under Security. To remove HERO's Apple sign-in connection, open Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings (on iPhone: Settings, your name, Sign-In & Security) and remove HERO. Neither step deletes your HERO account, so also sign out in HERO, and use Delete my account if you want the account removed.

Apple Health

In Recovery data, choose Connect Apple Health in a compatible installed HERO iOS app with Health access. Access is optional and read-only. You choose whether HERO may read sleep, resting heart rate, HRV (SDNN), steps and workouts. HERO writes no Health records.

Imported summaries stay in app memory and are excluded from your saved plan and account sync. An empty result can mean that no readable records are available. Review permissions in Apple Health or Settings, keep the device unlocked and try reading again. Clear imported data removes the displayed summaries without deleting the original Health records or revoking permission.

Calendar tools

Choose a calendar, review the week's busy intervals and select which times to add to your plan. HERO saves selected intervals with the generic title Calendar commitment. Original event titles, source identifiers, guests, notes and locations are not saved in the plan. Later source-calendar changes do not update imported commitments.

Calendar export opens an editor for you to review and save a commitment. Check your calendar to confirm the result. These tools do not provide automatic two-way sync.

Evidence and research questions

Ask HERO finds related evidence on your device. Try a topic such as sleep, magnesium or travel. You can save an article, read its limitations and follow links to original sources. Research-team contact opens a draft in your email app for you to review and send.

HERO provides general research education and planning tools. Contact your clinician for personal medical questions.

Optional Ask HERO AI answers

In builds with AI answers enabled, sign in to HERO and enter a question between 10 and 2,000 characters. Each question or retry asks for permission to send the question and selected public evidence to OpenAI through the HERO backend. Review the question and privacy notice before agreeing. Canceling sends nothing. Finding related evidence remains available without sending an AI request.

AI answers can make mistakes. Open the cited articles and read their limitations. HERO cannot choose a diagnosis, treatment, supplement dose or personal recovery protocol for you. An AI answer does not contact the research team, and support email is not a crisis service.

If you reach a usage limit, wait before trying again. Retrying can count toward the limit. If AI answers are paused or temporarily unavailable, you can still use the evidence library. If a problem persists, contact support with the message shown in HERO, without including sensitive medical details.

Optional WHOOP connection

WHOOP requires separate HERO and WHOOP accounts and a build with the connection enabled. Sign in to HERO, open Recovery data and choose Connect WHOOP, then Continue to WHOOP. Review the read permissions in WHOOP, complete authorization and return to HERO. Use Check connection to verify the saved status before importing recent data. Canceling an authorization does not confirm a new connection.

Import recent WHOOP data or Refresh WHOOP data brings recent summaries into the current app session. Wait briefly between refresh attempts. Missing or unscored information can remain blank. Imported summaries are separate from your saved plan and AI questions. Clear imported WHOOP data removes the displayed summaries; it does not disconnect WHOOP or delete the original WHOOP records.

Disconnect WHOOP and revoke access attempts to revoke access. A confirmed disconnect removes the saved HERO connection. If HERO cannot confirm revocation, follow its message and retry. If it asks you to reconnect, choose Renew WHOOP access while a connection is saved, or Connect WHOOP if no connection is saved, and approve the requested permissions. You can also remove HERO directly in WHOOP's connected-app settings. Do not treat a failed disconnect or an empty imported view as confirmation that access was revoked.

New WHOOP connections are subject to provider approval, member capacity and temporary pauses. A limit message can also mean that an update is already running or that requests are too close together. Wait and retry once; if the message persists, contact support. A HERO account does not guarantee an available WHOOP connection slot.
