The question

Does the formulation
make a difference?

HERO Lab is preparing a randomized, blinded study comparing two magnesium formulations with a matching placebo. The study will follow sleep and recovery in athletes’ daily lives and measure performance through structured testing.

Our primary question is whether sleep efficiency changes from the baseline week to the final treatment week. Recovery and performance measures are secondary outcomes.

Participants are randomly assigned in equal proportions. The study is designed to test these questions; results are not yet available.

The protocol

Five weeks of observation.

Baseline · 1 week

Establish the starting point.

Baseline wearable monitoring provides a reference for each athlete’s sleep and recovery measures.

Treatment · 4 weeks

Nightly capsules. Continuous monitoring.

Participants take their assigned study capsules nightly during the treatment period. WHOOP 5.0 monitoring continues, alongside the study’s performance assessments.

Study visits, eligibility, supplement instructions, and participation requirements are confirmed by the study team.

What we measure

Sleep at the center.
Recovery and performance in view.

Primary outcome

Sleep efficiency

Change in WHOOP-derived sleep efficiency from the baseline week to the final treatment week.

Secondary outcomes

Recovery + performance

  • WHOOP sleep and recovery measures, including HRV and Recovery Score
  • Reaction-time testing
  • VALD grip-strength and countermovement-jump testing

Wearable measures and performance tests answer different questions. These planned outcomes are not evidence of a treatment benefit.
