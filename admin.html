Reading the reports

Custom events include study-link clicks, inquiry-link clicks, film downloads, and film engagement. Inquiry clicks do not confirm that an email was sent or a participant enrolled. Film progress measures footage actually watched; seeking to the end does not count as completion.

Google also collects standard session and engagement information after consent. Advertising features and enhanced measurement are disabled. Visitors who decline, block analytics, or use Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are excluded, so these reports do not represent every visitor.

Search Console independently reports activity from Google Search. Its clicks and impressions are different measures from website visits and should not be added together.
