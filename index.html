Sleep and Reaction-Time Outcomes

Magnesium L-Threonate in NCAA Division I Athletes

A single-site, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial comparing 1 g of magnesium L-threonate compound nightly with rice-flour placebo for four weeks.

Primary Outcome
+0.7 min
Deep and slow-wave sleep at Week 4
MgT vs placebo · 95% CI −6.0 to 7.3 · p = .844 · n = 75
No evidence of benefit
Prespecified Secondary Outcome
52.3 ms
Greater reduction in RIW Challenge response latency
MgT vs placebo · 95% CI 22.7 to 81.9 · p < .001
Prespecified Secondary Outcome
34.2 ms
Greater reduction in Speed Tap response latency
MgT vs placebo · 95% CI 8.2 to 60.2 · p = .010
Reaction-Time Sample
107 athletes · MgT 53, placebo 54
Intervention
1 g MgT compound nightly
Duration
4 weeks
Monitoring
WHOOP plus pre/post testing

Unpublished results. The primary sleep outcome was null. Reaction-time findings were secondary outcomes and require replication.
