Human Enhancement Recovery Optimization

Make room for recovery.

HERO brings a weekly plan, research summaries and recovery commitments into one workspace for recreational athletes and busy professionals.

Your week

Organize work, training, sleep, travel and recovery. Review scheduling conflicts and protect time for sleep.

Evidence with context

Explore research summaries, their limitations and original sources. Save useful articles and add recovery commitments.

Optional Apple Health

Read sleep, resting heart rate, HRV (SDNN), steps and workouts in a compatible installed HERO iOS app with Health access. Imported summaries stay in app memory, separate from your saved plan.

A workspace of your own

Start with a guest workspace saved on your device. An optional HERO account keeps a separate workspace through Supabase, including the plans and manual check-ins you choose to save. Review account and sync status in the app.

Help and privacy

Read the support guide for account and connection help, or the privacy notice for data use and deletion choices.

HERO provides planning tools and general research education. It does not diagnose, treat or replace personal medical care.
