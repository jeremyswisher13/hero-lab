Website & newsletter privacy

Updated October 18, 2026

The HERO Brief newsletter

Signing up for The HERO Brief is voluntary. MailerLite collects your email address, your newsletter consent, and signup and email-confirmation information. We use MailerLite to maintain the subscriber list, send HERO Lab newsletter updates, and manage delivery status, unsubscribes, and complaints. New website signups require email confirmation. Newsletter signup is separate from research participation and does not enroll you in a study. Please do not include health information or personal medical details in the signup form or newsletter replies.

Loading the signup page contacts MailerLite to display and operate the form. MailerLite processes connection information, including your IP address, and may use cookies or browser storage for form sessions and submission state. These requests occur independently of your separate optional website analytics choice. See MailerLite's privacy policy and form-cookie information. Subscriber records are private and are not published on this website. We do not send newsletter email addresses or form entries to Google Analytics.

If you allow optional website analytics and arrive through an approved HERO Brief signup link, the form can send MailerLite a fixed signup-source label, such as instagram_story or event_talk, with your signup. We keep only the approved source label and expiry information in this tab's session storage for up to 30 minutes, so browsing other public pages does not lose the source. A new approved campaign replaces it; invalid campaign links, expired permission, or withdrawing permission clear it. Untagged visits without a valid saved source use not_recorded. Separately, approved website signup links can record a fixed placement label, such as home_hero or travel_inline, in MailerLite. A website placement does not establish which outside channel brought you here. These labels describe the signup journey and may change if you submit again. We do not store or send full links, referring pages, email addresses, or arbitrary URL values as attribution data. The labels become part of your private MailerLite subscriber record; withdrawing analytics permission stops future attribution and clears this tab's saved source, but does not delete previously saved subscriber labels.

For earlier signups through Google Forms, we retain the original signup source, consent wording, and submission time privately. Those historical responses remain protected by Google account permissions; the Google Form is no longer this website's signup route. Google handles the retained records under its privacy policy.

Open tracking is enabled for newsletter emails delivered through MailerLite. An invisible beacon in the email can report an open associated with your subscription. MailerLite also records clicks on newsletter links associated with your subscription. These email open and link-click metrics are separate from your choice about optional website analytics below.

Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe link. You can also request removal at any time by emailing jswisher@mednet.ucla.edu with “HERO Brief unsubscribe” in the subject. We retain your signup while you remain subscribed and keep a limited suppression record after you unsubscribe so that a later list import does not add you back.

Optional website analytics

HERO Lab uses Google Analytics on its public website only when you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not affect access to the website, research information, study contacts, or videos. This page does not load analytics.

What is measured

With permission, we measure page visits, clicks on selected study and contact links, film downloads, and video engagement. On the newsletter signup page, we also measure when a valid signup request is sent to MailerLite (hero_newsletter_submit) and when MailerLite reports that it accepted that request (hero_newsletter_accepted). Each stage is counted at most once per page visit, only if analytics was active when the request began and permission still applies. Acceptance can include an address already on the list and does not mean the email was confirmed or a new subscriber was added. We do not measure confirmed subscriptions in Google Analytics. A contact-link click indicates interest; it does not tell us whether you sent an email or enrolled in a study.

Google Analytics uses cookies and collects browser, device, session, and interaction information. Google processes network information, including your IP address, when receiving requests. We configure the site to send fixed public page names and URLs without query strings or fragments, and omit referring-page URLs. We do not send email addresses, message contents, form entries, or research records in our custom events.

With analytics permission, visits from approved HERO Brief newsletter links also include fixed campaign labels, such as hero_brief_2026_10 for October 2026 and hero_brief_2026_11 for November 2026, with source newsletter and medium email. Only an exact, unique approved campaign link qualifies. Other URL query values, including dates and subscriber identifiers, are not sent. On the supported public pages, exact approved signup links can also add fixed recruitment campaign labels: campaign hero_brief_signup, with approved Instagram, email-signature, or event/QR placements. We read these query values only after analytics permission and send only their approved fixed labels. Only a current approved campaign link adds these campaign labels to Google events. A source retained during an internal browsing journey can still accompany a signup in MailerLite, but is not used to invent a Google campaign for an untagged page. Website placement labels are kept separate from campaign attribution. Signup stage events contain fixed page and campaign labels, without email addresses or other form entries. MailerLite email activity and confirmed subscriptions remain separate from these consented website events.

Advertising features, Google signals, and automatic enhanced measurement are disabled. Analytics is excluded from the lab workspace, administration, preview, and other unlisted pages. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences keep the public-site analytics off.

Your choice

The site stores your analytics choice in your browser for up to 180 days. With permission, an approved signup source may also remain in this tab's session storage for up to 30 minutes, without extending that period on ordinary page visits. If you allow analytics, its cookies also expire after up to 180 days without extending their expiry on each visit. You can change your choice using Analytics preferences in the footer of public research pages or the newsletter signup page.

Choosing Decline after previously allowing analytics disables collection, removes this site's analytics cookies, and reloads the page to stop the analytics script. Previously collected information is not deleted by changing this browser preference. If your browser blocks preference storage, we will ask again on a later page.
