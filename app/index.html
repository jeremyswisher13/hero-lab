Optional Google Calendar connection

Google Calendar is currently offered through a limited pilot while public access is being verified. With your permission, HERO lists your calendars and reads events from the calendars you choose, or your primary calendar until you choose. It adds generic commitments and suggests travel plans for your review. HERO never writes to Google Calendar. Calendar permission is separate from Google sign-in.
