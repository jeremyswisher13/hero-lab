Inside HERO Lab

Stay close to the research.

HERO Lab brings together sports medicine investigators from UCLA and UCSF. Our work combines clinical questions, wearable monitoring, and performance testing in collegiate athletes.

The HERO Brief follows that work and the wider literature. We explain study design, findings, and uncertainty, and link to the original evidence so you can read further.

About your subscription

Can I read an issue before subscribing?

Yes. Read the October 13 issue for a look at the writing, research links, and limitations we include. Our newsletter archive contains issues that have been sent to subscribers.

What is in each issue of The HERO Brief?

A short update on HERO Lab's work, three research reads with findings and limitations, and one tool, product, or practical resource worth a look.

Who is The HERO Brief for?

Athletes, coaches, athletic trainers, and sports medicine professionals. You can follow the main findings or use the source links to examine the research in more detail.

How often will I receive it, and what does it cost?

The HERO Brief is free and arrives once a month, on the second Tuesday. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each issue.

How do I finish signing up?

Enter your email in the signup form, select the permission checkbox, and submit. Then open the confirmation email from MailerLite and use its confirmation link to activate your subscription. If it is missing, check your spam folder.
